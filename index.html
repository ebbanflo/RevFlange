Revolver is a flanger built around an envelope-controlled delay line. The central rotary mechanism sweeps through comb filtering in two modes — LFO for continuous modulation, Manual for static placement — while the envelope section shapes how the effect responds to incoming signal.

Feedback pushes the comb into resonance. Stereo spreads the two channels apart in time, turning mono flanging into something that moves through a room. Mix blends wet and dry without affecting the envelope tracking.

Unsigned AU. macOS arm64. Built with JUCE.
